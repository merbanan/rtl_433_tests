# CAME Atomo sample

Signal generated from the CAME Atomo protocol with a made-up serial and transmitted by a
Flipper Zero as a RAW file, recorded with an RTL-SDR at 433.92 MHz, 250 kHz, RSSI about -9 dB,
SNR about 30 dB. One button press, 8 parcels with hold counters 10, 30, 125, 126, 127, 0, 1, 3.
`protocol` selects the CAME Atomo decoder.

| File | Serial | Button | Press counter |
|---|---|---|---|
| `button_2_433.92M_250k.cu8` | `7E21C04B` | 2 | 291 |
